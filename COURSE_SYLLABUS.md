# Syllabus — Statistics for Data Science, AI & ML

## Prerequisites
Basic algebra, reading graphs, and introductory Python. Provide a short Python refresher for learners who need it.

## Learning objectives
Students will interpret distributions, quantify uncertainty, evaluate evidence, fit and diagnose basic regression models, and reason statistically about ML evaluation and experiments.

## Weekly sequence (adaptable)
1. Data literacy and descriptive statistics
2. Visualization, quantiles, robust summaries, outliers
3. Probability rules and conditional probability
4. Bayes' theorem and base rates
5. Random variables, expectation and variance
6. Common distributions
7. Sampling distributions and CLT
8. Confidence intervals and estimation
9. Hypothesis tests and effect sizes
10. Comparing groups and multiple testing
11. Correlation and regression
12. Regression diagnostics and assumptions
13. ML metrics, leakage, uncertainty
14. Bootstrap and Bayesian updating
15. A/B tests and power concepts
16. Capstone presentations and review

## Required student habits
- Explain each result in a sentence.
- Label axes and state units.
- State the target population and sampling process.
- Keep a record of exclusions and transformations.
- Distinguish statistical significance from practical importance.
